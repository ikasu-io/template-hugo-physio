**This practice does not exist** — the points below show what a real
practice describes here.

This website sets no cookies, loads no fonts, maps or scripts from other
servers and has no contact form. When you visit it, the web server keeps
the usual access data (IP address, time, page requested) for at most
seven days to keep the site running safely.

If you email us, we use what you send only to answer your request and
plan your appointment. Please do not email us health records; bring them
to your first appointment instead.

A real practice also names the person responsible, the hosting provider
and your rights to access, correction and deletion here.
