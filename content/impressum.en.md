**This practice does not exist.** Ostkai Physio is a made-up practice
for a demo website; the people, the address and the services are
invented. A real German practice lists here what the Digital Services
Act (§ 5 DDG) requires:

- the name and address of the practice or its owner
- an email address and a second quick way to get in touch
- the professional title (physiotherapist) and the country that granted it
- the responsible supervisory authority (e.g. the public health office)
- the VAT identification number, if there is one

The website was generated with AI and is maintained with
[Ikasu](https://ikasu.io).
